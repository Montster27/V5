# Product Context

## Purpose
- Time-travel RPG where players relive college years with future knowledge
- Targets 50-70 age demographic
- Combines therapeutic benefits with entertainment

## Problems Solved
- Provides cognitive engagement for older adults
- Creates meaningful nostalgia experience
- Delivers therapeutic benefits through gameplay

## Core Functionality
- Time Management (1 day per 3 seconds)
- Resource Management (energy, stress, money, knowledge, social)
- Event System (daily events, pivotal moments, mysteries)
- Character Development through choices and resource management
